An evolving collection of ideas for a Minecraft MMORPG on a Purpur 26.1.1 server. Every idea is meant to be buildable with plugins and a server resource pack, with no client mods. They are concepts, not a promise that each will ship.

Plugins only

Quests, skills, parties, guilds, regions, menus, economy, custom items, instanced dungeons.

Plus a resource pack

Custom item models, weapons, capes, icons, sounds and music, menu art.

Not realistic without client mods

New player animations, shaders, a free camera, a fully custom HUD, physics beyond vanilla.

Effort, as a rough guide: Easy a small plugin feature or build work. Medium a feature that needs its own system. Hard a large system that needs design, scripting and testing.
